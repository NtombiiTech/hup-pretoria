Wikimedia provenance cites official SAPS publication https://www.saps.gov.za/resource_centre/publications/downloads/cover_NEA_2017.pdf . The vector is a third-party transcription of the official badge, not a file directly downloaded from SAPS. Direct SAPS page downloads returned 503.

The three raster sources are embedded unchanged in SVG presentation wrappers, with SVG light-background filtering. No logo shapes or lettering have been generated or redrawn. Names accompany each mark. SVG transparency is a display effect rather than a newly supplied official transparent variant. The marks are linked to their respective official sites.
